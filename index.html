A BUG in the Capital region of Finland, established 2017.

Capital of Finland being Helsinki, naturally our BUG is named HELBUG.

HELBUG's goal is to promote the use of the BSD variants and the knowledge relating to this.

Helsinki area BSD User Groups (HELBUG's) plan is to have semiregular meetings with varying agendas.

These will include talks, seminars, hacking workshops and general discussion on BSD variants.
